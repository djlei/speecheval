People who help us
There are lots of people who help us
at home, at school and outside.

Some people who help us wear uniforms.
Uniforms are special clothes people wear
for their work.

Ben is a firefighter. He needs to
wear a black uniform for his job.
He sometimes works in the
day, and sometimes at night.
Some weeks he works on
Saturdays and Sundays. He gets
up every day at six o'clock.

He has breakfast
and a shower at the
fire station where he
works. Sometimes he
even sleeps there!

Every day is different for
Ben. Firefighters help lots of
people. They stop fires in houses
and other places, they help people in
accidents and sometimes they even
help animals on farms.

Some days Ben goes to schools to
talk to children about how to be safe.
